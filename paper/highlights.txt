Highlights

In the evaluated systems, about 25% of matched elicitation pairs disagree on the investment-grade/high-yield classification.
The elicitation axes jointly explain little rating variance (R² 0.083–0.119). The fractional design does not identify per-axis shares. Pure seed noise is ~8%.
Instability persists on the temperature-0-honored subgrid (one provider, temperature 0).
Unweighted cross-specification agreement stays modest (Fleiss κ≤ 0.49).
At the binary IG/HY gate, a dispersion index predicts held-out instability (ROC-AUC 0.94) but is ordinally equivalent to dissent count, so it is reported descriptively.
